Complaint Template (Narrative-Use Claims)

Overview

This template converts the Narrative Use Claim Assembly Protocol into a filing-ready complaint structure.

It is designed to:

(1) maximize plausibility at L (12(b)(6))
(2) leverage public data (PDIM)
(3) establish USE → value → misalignment


⸻

I. Caption

[COURT NAME]

[PLAINTIFF NAME],
    Plaintiff,

v.                                              Civil Action No. ______

[DEFENDANT NAME],
    Defendant.


⸻

II. Introduction

1. This case arises from Defendant’s use of a publicly identifiable narrative
   to generate value through institutional action.

2. Plaintiff alleges that Defendant operationalized this narrative to obtain
   [funding / authority / legitimacy], and that the resulting value was not
   allocated in alignment with that use.

3. The facts supporting these allegations are drawn from publicly available
   records, including [identify sources], and are sufficient to state a
   plausible claim for relief.


⸻

III. Jurisdiction and Venue

4. This Court has jurisdiction under [statute].

5. Venue is proper in this district because [facts].

6. Defendant conducts business and/or governmental activity within this district.


⸻

IV. Parties

7. Plaintiff [describe identity and connection to narrative].

8. Defendant [describe entity, role, and authority].

9. At all relevant times, Defendant acted [in official capacity / as a commercial actor].


⸻

V. Factual Allegations

A. The Narrative (N)

10. The narrative at issue concerns [describe narrative clearly and specifically].

11. This narrative is publicly recognizable and has been referenced in
    [historical records / public discourse / institutional materials].


⸻

B. Defendant’s Use of the Narrative (USE)

12. Defendant publicly invoked and operationalized this narrative through
    [specific actions].

13. These actions include, but are not limited to:

    (a) [public statement / program launch]
    (b) [policy justification]
    (c) [funding request or allocation]

14. These actions are documented in public records, including:

    (a) [press releases]
    (b) [government publications]
    (c) [grant or funding documents]


⸻

C. Value Generated (V)

15. As a result of this use, Defendant obtained value, including:

    (a) financial resources, including [amounts if known]
    (b) institutional authority or expansion
    (c) reputational or public legitimacy

16. This value is reflected in [budget records / announcements / outcomes].


⸻

D. Distribution of Value

17. The value generated from Defendant’s use of the narrative was allocated to:

    [identify recipients or uses]

18. These allocations are documented in [public records].


⸻

E. Misalignment

19. Defendant’s use of the narrative generated value that was not allocated
    in alignment with that narrative.

20. Specifically, while Defendant relied on the narrative to obtain value,
    the resulting benefits were directed to [describe misalignment].

21. This misalignment is observable from publicly available data and does not
    depend on information exclusively within Defendant’s control.


⸻

VI. Plausibility Allegations (L Layer)

22. Plaintiff’s allegations are supported by specific factual content drawn
    from publicly available sources.

23. These facts establish a plausible relationship between:

    (a) Defendant’s use of the narrative,
    (b) the value obtained by Defendant, and
    (c) the allocation of that value.

24. Accordingly, Plaintiff has stated a claim that is plausible on its face.


⸻

VII. Causes of Action (Example Structure)

Count I — [Insert Legal Theory]

25. Plaintiff incorporates all preceding paragraphs.

26. Defendant used the narrative as part of its conduct to obtain value.

27. Defendant retained or directed that value in a manner inconsistent with
    that use.

28. As a result, Plaintiff has been harmed in a legally cognizable manner.

(Repeat structure for additional counts as needed)

⸻

VIII. Prayer for Relief

WHEREFORE, Plaintiff respectfully requests that the Court:

(a) enter judgment in favor of Plaintiff;

(b) award damages in an amount to be determined;

(c) order appropriate equitable relief;

(d) grant such other relief as the Court deems just and proper.


⸻

IX. Jury Demand (if applicable)

Plaintiff demands a trial by jury on all issues so triable.


⸻

X. Signature Block

Respectfully submitted,

[Attorney Name]
[Bar Number]
[Address]
[Phone]
[Email]


⸻

XI. Structural Notes (Not Filed)

- Every key allegation should tie USE → value → public fact
- Avoid conclusory statements without supporting data
- Use public records to stabilize plausibility
- Maintain tight linkage between narrative and value generation


⸻

XII. Final Compression

Narrative
→ USE
→ Public Data
→ Plausibility
→ Survive
→ Evaluate


⸻

One-Line Definition

A complaint is a structured document that uses public facts to establish a plausible account of narrative use, value generation, and misalignment sufficient to proceed to adjudication.
